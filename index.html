Commonwealth Radio

Music around the clock out of Midoriheiki. Pre-selected playlists run when there's no DJ. When a DJ connects, the studio sign turns red and the show is theirs until they sign off. Anyone can put a song from the library in the queue from the request line.

Want the music to follow you around the site, or to keep it going with this tab closed? Press POP-UP on the player.

Program guide
Waiting for the transmitter.

The next seven days, in your own clock. LIVE slots are DJs. The rest are Auto DJ playlists. Any empty hour plays the general rotation. A DJ who connects outside their slot takes the air anyway.

About the station

Tanuki Broadcasting is a private broadcasting corporation founded in Midoriheiki. The station is the company's, and not a government service. All opinions expressed by employees do not reflect that of the Commonwealth government in any way, shape or form.

The Commonwealth is a monarchy on CivMC, founded 6 June 2022, with its capital at Mount Olympus near -4000, 0. It governs through a House of Commons, and a Prime Minister, while having a House of Lords that act as semi-independent regional governors. Its motto is To Live Without Fear – Vivre Sans Peur. The Commonwealth has historically acted as a haven for newfriends, and grown scions into beautiful trees. The rest of the nation's story is on its CivWiki article.
